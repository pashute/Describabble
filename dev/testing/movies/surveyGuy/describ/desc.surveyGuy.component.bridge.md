## Suspension Bridge Setup

**Bridge Type:** Suspension bridge (see meta.gen.component.bridge)  
**Setting:** Over a river, daytime, clear weather  
**Ambience:** Fast-moving traffic in distance (not loud, ambient background)  

## Bridge Structure Elements

**Deck/Road:**
- Horizontal line at mid-frame
- Single line suffices (minimal stick style)
- Represents the walking surface

**Fence Top Rail:**
- Horizontal line above the deck
- Runs full length of visible span
- Provides railing visual

**Fence Poles:**
- Short vertical lines between rail and road
- Placed at regular intervals
- Evenly spaced across frame
- Create visual railing texture

**Suspension Cable (Catenary):**
- Curved line above fence rail
- Arcs downward (catenary curve, not perfectly straight)
- Conveys suspension system and structure
- Creates sense of height when viewed from below

**Suspension Hangers:**
- Vertical lines from catenary to top rail
- Evenly spaced with fence poles
- Connect cable system to deck
- Minimal, thin lines for minimalism

**River/Ground:**
- Horizontal line far below bridge
- Implied or explicitly shown depending on POV
- Creates visual distance and scale

**Sky/Background:**
- Empty space or light color
- Not detailed (stick style minimalism)

## Scale & Proportion

**Overall Proportion:**
- Bridge dominates frame when viewed from below (Riverside POV)
- Creates sense of isolation and height for Survey Guy
- Bridge minimized when viewed from bridge level (Bridge POV)

**Character Size Relative to Bridge:**
- Survey Guy appears small against full bridge structure (Riverside POV)
- Emphasizes vulnerability and significance of moment
- Bridge POV shows structure minimal (camera positioned on deck looking down)

**Fence Height:**
- Fence rail typically reaches mid-point to upper-body of stick figure
- Emphasizes climbing difficulty (reaching over fence)

## Visual Details for Specificity

**Minimal Hints:**
- Cables frame character from below (Riverside POV)
- Character surrounded by structural elements
- Creates "trapped" or "exposed" visual composition

**Color/Texture (if applicable):**
- Black lines on light background (standard stick figure style)
- No color variation needed (all black structural elements)
- Can add blue for river below (optional, enhances clarity)

## POV-Specific Framing

**Riverside POV (Scenes with Survey Guy):**
- Camera at river/ground level
- Bridge structure full and prominent above
- Deck, rails, poles, cables all visible
- Survey Guy positioned climbing or standing on fence
- Cables frame view (visual composition)

**Bridge POV (Scenes with Worried Man):**
- Camera on bridge level
- Ground/river far below
- Minimal bridge structure visible (edges implied)
- Worried Man standing below, appearing small
- Emphasizes looking down over edge

## Emotional Impact Through Bridge Design

**Isolation:**
- Bridge height creates physical distance between characters
- Structural elements frame each character separately
- No escape route visually (bridge path continues only up/down)

**Desperation:**
- Bridge dominance (from Riverside POV) shows height and consequence
- Cables and structure create sense of weight above
- Limited space emphasizes trapped feeling

**Decision Point:**
- Bridge serves as literal crossroads (stay or act)
- Visual structure supports thematic weight
- Scale and perspective convey stakes

## Animation Considerations

**Static Structure:**
- Bridge remains fixed throughout scene (no animation)
- Characters move within/relative to bridge elements

**Camera Positioning:**
- Two fixed POVs (Riverside and Bridge)
- Clean cuts between POVs (no transitions)
- Frame composition maintains visual balance
