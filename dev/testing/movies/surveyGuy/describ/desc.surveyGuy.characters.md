## Survey Guy (JMP)

**Role:** Lead protagonist  
**Age:** 30s  
**Gender:** Male  

**Appearance:**
- Stick figure (see meta.gen.stick.emotions for base structure)
- Black hair line above head (short, simple)
- Bent tie hanging diagonally on chest (primary distinguishing feature)

**Voice:**
- Gender: Male
- Age: 30s
- Quality: Slightly nasal, phone rep characteristic
- Delivery: Moderately paced, rapid when distressed

**Mood Arc (0-53s):**
- **0-4s:** Distressed, climbing hesitantly, panting audible
- **4-14s:** Defensive, responds sharply, denying family connections
- **14-26s:** Raw, becoming more honest, voice cracks
- **26-40s:** Anguished, bitter, complaining about job
- **40-46s:** Contemplative, processing Worried Man's logic
- **46-53s:** Accepting, considers the offer, then implied acceptance

**Posture States:**
- Climbing (0-4s): Body angled 45° upward, arms extended gripping, legs bent, head down
- Standing on Ledge (4-40s): Upright on fence rail, arms for balance, looking toward river
- Thinking (40-46s): Still, upright, slight slouch, eyes/head adjusting
- After Punchline (46-51s): Body stable, standing, contemplative

**Emotions (See trans.surveyGuy.emotions.md for details):**
- Panting: Open mouth with breath lines, exhaustion in posture
- Defensive: Straight mouth, widened eyes, body tense
- Raw/Honest: Mouth open or tight, eyes engaged, body relaxed slightly
- Anguished: Mouth open (O shape), distressed posture, body tension
- Thinking: Subtle smile or neutral mouth, eyes directed away, slight head tilt
- Accepting: Subtle expression, resigned posture, body settles

## Worried Man (WM)

**Role:** Support character  
**Age:** 24  
**Gender:** Male  

**Appearance:**
- Stick figure (see meta.gen.stick.emotions for base structure)
- Elongated vertical oval head (distinguishing feature: "long face")
- Simple stick limbs, no accessories

**Voice:**
- Gender: Male
- Age: 24, younger
- Quality: Clear, direct, urgent
- Delivery: Fast during pleas, moderate during reasoning, clear for punchline

**Mood Arc (0-53s):**
- **9-14s:** Worried, urgent first plea (wife/children)
- **19-23s:** Desperate, escalating second plea (parents)
- **26-30s:** Peak desperation, third plea (future)
- **40-46s:** Logical, reasoning pivot to practical advice
- **46-51s:** Cunning, delivering punchline with measured clarity

**Posture States:**
- Looking Up (9-30s): Standing on ground, head tilted back, arms may gesture upward
- Persistent (30-46s): Standing firm, body upright, arms gesture for emphasis
- Punchline (46-51s): Confident posture, direct stance, subtle knowing expression

**Emotions (See trans.surveyGuy.emotions.md for details):**
- Worried: Widened eyes, downward eyebrows, mouth straight or wavy
- Desperate: Wide eyes, exaggerated mouth shapes, body tense
- Logical: Straight mouth, engaged eyes, measured posture
- Cunning: Subtle smile, knowing look, controlled body stance
- Shouting: Mouth open wide, high volume, body forward-leaning

## Character Interaction

- **Physical Distance:** Bridge height apart (significant gap)
- **Visibility:** Never both characters visible simultaneously (alternating POVs)
- **Communication:** Shouting across distance, clear enunciation required
- **Eye Contact:** Implied through POV switching and dialogue direction
- **Dialogue Pattern:** Call-and-response, back-and-forth escalation
