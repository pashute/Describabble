## Emotion Translations

### Survey Guy Emotions

#### Distressed (0-4s Climbing)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Distressed  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Distressed  
- Mouth: Open (O shape) or wide exhalation lines (panting)
- Eyes: Widened or regular dots
- Eyebrows: Slightly raised (fear element)
- Head: Forward tilt (down while climbing)
- Body: Angled upward 45°, arms raised gripping
- Breathing: Audible panting in audio layer

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Climbing  
**Duration:** 0-4s  
**Visual Impact:** Exhaustion, effort, fear of task  

#### Defensive (4-14s First Response)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Confident (defensive variation)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Worried (hardened)  
- Mouth: Straight dash or slight downward curve
- Eyes: Widened dots or focused
- Eyebrows: Angled or neutral (determined)
- Head: Upright or tilted back (shouting)
- Body: Upright on fence, rigid posture
- Voice: Shouting, emphatic, defensive

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (rigid)  
**Duration:** 4-14s  
**Visual Impact:** Defiance, self-protection through denial  

#### Raw/Honest (14-26s Second Response)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Anguished (vulnerable)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Anguished (medium intensity)  
- Mouth: Open or trembling
- Eyes: Widened, possibly glistening
- Eyebrows: Angled downward (sadness)
- Head: Upright or slight forward tilt
- Body: Upright but posture settling
- Voice: Cracking slightly, raw delivery

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (vulnerable)  
**Duration:** 14-26s  
**Visual Impact:** Truth-telling, emotional breakthrough  

#### Anguished/Bitter (26-40s Job Complaint)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Anguished (high) + Angry (frustrated)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Distressed or Anguished  
- Mouth: Open (shouting) or mouth shapes changing
- Eyes: Wide or intense
- Eyebrows: Furrowed (anger element)
- Head: Upright, may move with emphasis
- Body: Upright with body tension (arms may gesture)
- Voice: Shouting, bitter, sarcastic tone

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (emphatic gesturing)  
**Duration:** 26-40s  
**Visual Impact:** Despair through job complaint, self-awareness  

#### Contemplative (40-46s Reasoning Pause)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Thinking (internal processing)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Thinking  
- Mouth: Subtle curve or neutral dash
- Eyes: Dots looking away or upward (thinking)
- Eyebrows: Neutral or slight upward (interest)
- Head: Slight tilt to side or back (thinking pose)
- Body: Upright but relaxed
- Posture: Still, minimal movement
- Silence: No speaking (implied pause)

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (idle, thoughtful)  
**Duration:** 40-46s (implied, within Worried Man's speaking)  
**Visual Impact:** Internal struggle, considering new perspective  

#### Accepting (46-53s End)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Resigned (acceptance of fate)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Resigned or Neutral  
- Mouth: Subtle curve or neutral
- Eyes: Settling or looking toward decision
- Eyebrows: Neutral or slightly relaxed
- Head: Upright, centered
- Body: Upright, posture settles
- Posture: Acceptance evident in stillness
- Silence: No speaking (decision made)

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (settled)  
**Duration:** 46-53s  
**Visual Impact:** Acceptance, decision made, ready for action  

### Worried Man Emotions

#### Worried (9-14s First Plea)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Worried  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Worried  
- Mouth: Straight or slight downward curve
- Eyes: Widened dots
- Eyebrows: Angled downward toward center (inverted V)
- Head: Tilted up (looking toward bridge)
- Body: Upright, standing firm
- Posture: Arms may gesture upward or outward (appealing)
- Voice: Shouting, urgent

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing + Gesturing  
**Duration:** 9-14s  
**Visual Impact:** Concern, urgency, attempting to reach  

#### Desperate (19-30s Escalating Pleas)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Desperate (high intensity)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Distressed (exaggerated)  
- Mouth: Open wide or variations
- Eyes: Very wide, possibly with intensity marks
- Eyebrows: Strongly angled downward
- Head: Tilted up, may move with emotion
- Body: Rigid, tension visible
- Posture: Arms extended or dramatic (emphatic gesturing)
- Voice: Shouting at peak intensity

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (emphatic)  
**Duration:** 19-30s  
**Visual Impact:** Peak emotional escalation, urgency maximum  

#### Logical (40-46s Reasoning)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Confident (determined)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Thinking or Neutral  
- Mouth: Controlled, may be neutral or slight curve
- Eyes: Forward, direct (engaged, thinking)
- Eyebrows: Neutral or slight upward (confidence)
- Head: Upright, steady
- Body: Upright, posture firm
- Posture: Arms controlled, less gestural
- Voice: Shouting still but measured (not desperate)

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (controlled)  
**Duration:** 40-46s  
**Visual Impact:** Shift from emotion to logic, strategic change  

#### Cunning (46-51s Punchline)
**Generic Reference:** meta.gen.emotions → Amused (subtle humor)  
**Stick Rendering:** meta.gen.stick.emotions → Happy or Thinking (subtle)  
- Mouth: Subtle smile or controlled delivery
- Eyes: Direct, knowing (contact with idea if not person)
- Eyebrows: Neutral or slight upward (intelligence)
- Head: Upright, steady
- Body: Confident posture
- Posture: Arms controlled, composed
- Voice: Clear, measured (no longer shouting)

**Posture Reference:** meta.gen.stick.movement → Standing (confident, settled)  
**Duration:** 46-51s  
**Visual Impact:** Reveal of twist, dark humor redemption  

## Emotion Intensity Progression

### Survey Guy Intensity Arc
- 0-4s: Medium-High (exhaustion)
- 4-14s: High (defensive)
- 14-26s: Very High (raw anguish)
- 26-40s: Very High (bitter anguish)
- 40-46s: Low (thinking)
- 46-53s: Low (accepting)

**Overall:** High emotional intensity 4-40s, drop after reasoning begins

### Worried Man Intensity Arc
- 9-14s: High (first plea)
- 19-23s: Very High (second plea)
- 26-30s: Peak (third plea)
- 40-46s: Medium-Low (logical shift)
- 46-51s: Medium (controlled, clever)

**Overall:** Peaks at 26-30s (emotional climax), drops with logic pivot

## Emotion Transitions

**Abrupt Transitions:**
- Defensive to Raw: Between 14s (dialogue speaker change)
- Bitter to Contemplative: Between 40s (new input received)
- Urgent to Logical: Between 40s (Worried Man's topic change)

**Gradual Transitions:**
- Distressed to Defensive: 4s (settling while standing)
- Raw to Anguished: 14-26s (emotional escalation continuous)

**No Conflict/Simultaneous Emotions:**
- Survey Guy single emotion at any given time
- Worried Man single emotion at any given time
- Transitions happen at dialogue/speaker changes
