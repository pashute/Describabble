## POV Switching Rules

1. **Never Both Simultaneous:** Only one POV active at any time
2. **Clean Cut:** Switch POVs at dialogue speaker changes (no transitions)
3. **Speaker Stay:** POV stays with current speaker until dialogue ends
4. **No Mixed:** No intermediate angles or mixed perspectives
5. **Dialogue Lead:** Speaker determines POV, not location or camera logic
