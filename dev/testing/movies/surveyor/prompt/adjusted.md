## Screenplay

### EXT. SUSPENSION BRIDGE - DAY

**OPENING IMAGE:** Stickman movie black-and-white still image of suspension bridge made of sticks.

Fast-moving traffic is heard in the distance, almost silent. No honking.

The **SURVEYOR**, stick figure, climbs
hesitantly over the bridge fence. He wears a bent tie.
His panting is audible.

The camera moves in from behind him, holding the same angle as the bridge.

Below, on the riverside, the **WORRIED MAN**, a stickman with a long face, looks up. worried. 

The camera closes in from the Surveyor to the Worried Man.

**WORRIED MAN**  
(calling up)
Think about your wife! Think about your children!


RIVERSIDE POV: Close on the Surveyor looking down at the camera from the Worried Man's riverside angle; two suspension cables frame the background.

**THE SURVEYOR**  
(calling back)  
I don't have a wife! I don't have kids!


**BRIDGE POV:** From the bridge, a three-quarter-distance view of the Worried Man looking up.

**WORRIED MAN**  
(Shouts) Think about your parents!


RIVERSIDE POV
**THE SURVEYOR**  
(Shouts back)  I don't have parents!


BRIDGE POV
**WORRIED MAN**  
(Shouts) Think about your future!

RIVERSIDE POV
**THE SURVEYOR**  
(Shouts back) What future?! I have no future. I'm a surveyor.

A pause. 

**THE SURVEYOR (CONT'D)**  
(Shouts) Everybody hates surveys. They hear me talk and want to commit suicide!

RIVERSIDE POV
**WORRIED MAN**  
(clearly, shouting)
So change your job. You're not *that* stupid that you can't do anything else,
right?

Close on the Surveyor's face. His expression changes. He considers this.

The Surveyor begins to accept the idea. He thinks.


BRIDGE POV
The Worried Man calls up one last time.

**WORRIED MAN**  
(clearly, shouts)
Before you step down from the bridge, would you be willing to reply to a short survey?

**CUT TO BLACK.**

Loud splash sound heard.
