Describablle \- A Constructive Descriptive Video Framework

Version one (Distickable)  \- will stick to stick figure films. Then 2d animation and finally scenery. 

This new free movie maker framework runs from natura language instructions, with AI assistance along the way. 

There is no audio or video stored for these movies. They are all built out of descriptions being rendered in real time. 

The more descriptive input and precise rendering directions we provide, the closer we can get to identical, uniform, indistinguishable successive renders.

Audbabble(r) and Musociopath(r) together are the constructive descriptive audio and music frameworks which are an inseparable subsection of Describabble as described below.

\---

Details:

.desc files are shortest terse descriptions of things. 

They are created by AI from your prompts and are editable, by pausing a movie and correcting the connected description. 

There is no video file stored. Only an “implementation” layer which gives detailed terse natural language decisions (in yaml format?) for missing parameters, at any desired level of precision you require, so that the next render would be indistinguishable from the original. 

The things here are descriptions of : participants, scenery, artifacts, scenes, filming directives, the films conversation and background sounds, the music, descriptions of 3d objects and their components  etc. 

It can import parts from existing media such as films or 3d models, embedding them in the movie as given,  or converting them into Descirbabble and then rendering them as a movie. 

- Scenery has entities (trees, clouds, sky etc, in whatever detail we wish)   
    
- Screenplay parts:  Chapters, Action lines, Parenthetical directions, Transitions, Dialogs, Shots

- Shots can be described in detail: Camera info, movements, framing and scale etc etc.   
- 

Metabbable instructions \- are a set of useful shared instructions that can be pointed to, explaining how to read other describbable instructions. .  

For example  you can chose to point to the Irish Accent metababble, so that when you say Irish accent you get some details of how that is implemented.

\== Musociopath and Audbabble  \==

Musociopath \- describes music by player and phrases  with sync points, like a non-professional but capable player would explain their tune, rather than by time-first notation. 

But the descriptions may use any level of description text as long as it does not become non linguistic. So the muscriptions have minimal or no music tables or even chord progressions. Instead they only contain descriptive text with perhaps a few references to formal starting points if needed. 

The more details you provide, connecting each section to metabbable (in a process called metababbalism) the better the song rendering consistency will be. 

Audbabble describes the voices and their accents.  They describe how the lyrics sound rather than writing the words. So a lot of  oooh nd aaah.  And links to existing voices. 

A new and special UListenHear application is being built at Overstand Labs to listen to any voice and give a descriptive analysis that would be good enough to mimic an indistinguishable artificial voice on the fly.
